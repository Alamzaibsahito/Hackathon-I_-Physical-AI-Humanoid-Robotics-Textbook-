# AI-Powered Perception and Reinforcement Learning

## Learning Objectives
- Introduce computer vision for robot perception.
- Understand Reinforcement Learning (RL) in robotics.
- Grasp the challenges of "Sim-to-Real" transfer.

## AI Perception
Computer vision (e.g., Object Detection with YOLO) allows robots to identify obstacles, humans, or targets. In Physical AI, this perception feeds directly into the robot's planning and control system.

## Reinforcement Learning (RL)
RL teaches robots by trial-and-error. The robot interacts with an environment, receives rewards for desired actions (e.g., walking without falling), and updates its control policy to maximize long-term rewards.

## Sim-to-Real Challenges
Training RL models in simulation is great for safety, but models often fail when transferred to real robots due to the "sim-to-real gap"—the simulation is never 100% identical to the real, chaotic, noisy world.

## Takeaways
- Perception makes the world actionable; RL makes the robot adaptable.
- Sim-to-real gap is a fundamental hurdle in Physical AI.
