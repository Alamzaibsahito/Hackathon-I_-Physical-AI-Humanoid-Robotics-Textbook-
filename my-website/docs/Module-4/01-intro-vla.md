# Introduction to Vision-Language-Action Systems

## Learning Objectives
- Define VLA systems and their significance.
- Understand how LLMs and Robotics converge.
- See how VLA builds upon the foundations of previous modules.

## What is a VLA System?
A VLA system integrates **Vision** (understanding the environment), **Language** (comprehending natural language commands), and **Action** (executing robotic control) into a unified framework. It enables robots to perform complex tasks based on intuitive user requests.

## Convergence of AI and Robotics
This culmination represents the bridge from simple scripted behaviors to intelligent, reasoning, autonomous agents that can interpret human intent.
