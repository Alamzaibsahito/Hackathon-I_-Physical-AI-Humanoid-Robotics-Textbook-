# Introduction to Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence

## Learning Objectives
- Define Physical AI and distinguish it from traditional software-based AI.
- Understand the concept of "Embodied Intelligence".
- Identify key sensor types used in robotic systems.

## What is Physical AI?
Physical AI is the integration of artificial intelligence into physical hardware. While traditional AI (like large language models) lives in servers, Physical AI inhabits the real world. It allows robots to perceive, reason, and act in dynamic environments.

## Why Embodied Intelligence Matters
"Embodied Intelligence" refers to the idea that intelligence is not just a brain function, but is deeply tied to the body's interactions with the environment. A humanoid robot's intelligence is constrained and defined by its physical form—its limbs, motors, and sensors.

## Core Sensor Systems
- **LIDAR:** Uses laser pulses to create a 3D point cloud map.
- **Cameras (RGB-D):** Captures visual data and depth information.
- **IMUs:** Inertial Measurement Units track orientation, velocity, and gravitational forces.
- **Force/Torque Sensors:** Measures interaction forces at the joints and feet.

## Takeaways
- Physical AI closes the gap between computation and action.
- Embodiment is crucial for real-world interactions.
