# Introduction to Robot Simulation

## Learning Objectives
- Understand why simulation is essential before physical hardware deployment.
- Define the concept of a "Digital Twin".
- Get an overview of physics simulation engines.

## Why Simulation Matters
Simulating a robot allows you to test algorithms, experiment with control strategies, and train AI models safely, cheaply, and quickly. It catches bugs early, preventing costly hardware damage.

## The Digital Twin Concept
A "Digital Twin" is a virtual representation of a physical system. In robotics, this means a simulation model that behaves exactly like the real robot—matching its weight, kinematics, sensor response, and physical dynamics.

## Physics Engines
Physics engines (like ODE, Bullet, or PhysX) calculate how objects in the simulation should move and react, ensuring realism through simulated gravity, friction, and collisions.

## Takeaways
- Simulation is the bridge between software development and real-world deployment.
- Digital Twins are critical for testing and training.
